# Geometry of the window reveal

- wall thicknesses 36cm and 45 cm
- "Fensteranschlag" has around 50mm depth
- window position is: outer part of the outer frame is 15cm inside 
from the outer part of the wall. 
- Fensteranschlag and outer part of the wall are flush
- Window frames (sash and frame) can be assumed to be 70 mm wide
- windows are double glazed, U_g 1.1, U_w 1.3 (assume U_f 1.3 as well)
- ignore the spacers for now.

Three cases of insulation that we want to simulate:

1. no insulation
2. 200mm insulation (wls 035) that tapers down to 100mm at the edge of the window opening where the reveal goes into the wall. No insulation in the reveal.
3. Like 2 but with insulation in the reveal. This is 30mm thick with WLS 035.
