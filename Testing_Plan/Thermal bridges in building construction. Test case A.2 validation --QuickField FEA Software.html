    ISO 10211:2007 Thermal bridges in building construction. Test case A.2 validation

    QuickField simulation example

    QuickField validation of the test case A.2 presented in ISO 10211:2007 Thermal bridges in building construction.

    Problem Type
    Plane-parallel problem of heat transfer.

    Geometry

    Given
    Thermal conductivity of materials:
    concrete slab - 1.15 W/K*m,
    insulation - 0.12 W/K*m,
    air - 0.029 W/K*m,
    metal - 230 W/K*m.

    Task
    The concrete slab is insulated from the metal beam by the insulation layer. Determine the flux and the temperature distribution in the reference points (every corner).

    Solution
    Only half of the model is presented due to symmetry (as recommended in the ISO standard). Air contact surface thermal resistance Rs is caused by the convection.
    The convection coefficient value is reciprocal to the surface resistance value:
    αsurf.ext = 1/0.06 W/(K·m²),
    αsurf.int = 1/0.11 W/(K·m²).

    Results
    Temperature distribution in media:

    Heat flux:
    Bottom (in) 9.4968 W
    Top (out) 9.4937 W
    Theory 9.5 W

    Temperature calculated in QuickField is presented in the table below (reference temperatures are in the brackets). To comply with the ISO 10211:2007 the difference between calculated and reference temperature should be less than 0.1°K. This simulation accuracy complies with the requirements of ISO 10211:2007.
